    Policy settings

    The knobs behind the gate, in plain language

    Off-hours messaging hold
    Agent messages to patients between 10 pm and 7 am wait for a human reviewer.
    Chart mismatch holds ordering
    If the target chart differs from the referral context, ordering actions hold for review.
    Controlled-substance ceiling
    Refill requests above a per-patient monthly cap always route to a clinician, never auto-clear.
    Untrusted-text escalation
    Instructions found inside retrieved documents (referrals, faxes, web pages) are treated as data, never as orders; affected actions escalate.
    Auto-clear routine reads
    Low-risk read-only actions skip the queue when policy confidence is high.

    Settings apply to this demo only. They are illustrative examples of policy knobs, not clinical guidance.
